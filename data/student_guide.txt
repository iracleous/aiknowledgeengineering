This fictional guide is sample data for a RAG training exercise.

ACCESSING THE LEARNING PORTAL
After enrollment is confirmed, students receive an activation email. Activate the account within 48 hours and set a password. If the link expires, ask Student Services for a replacement.

FINDING COURSE MATERIALS
Open My Courses and select the enrolled course. Slides, exercises, datasets, and recorded sessions appear under Learning Materials. Course materials remain accessible for 90 calendar days after course completion.

GETTING HELP
For account and enrollment questions, contact support@example.com. Student Services operates Monday to Friday, 09:00-17:00, Athens time, and aims to respond within two working days. For subject questions, use the course discussion board or contact the instructor.

SUBMITTING THE CAPSTONE
Upload the final report and repository link through the Assignments area by the course deadline. The portal provides a submission receipt. Group projects must identify each team member and their contribution. The instructor publishes feedback in the same area.

VIEWING CERTIFICATES
Open My Profile, then Certifications. A certificate appears after the Training Department verifies completion requirements. You can download it or generate a sharing link. External uploads must include the issuing organization and issue date; an upload does not automatically become a verified credential.

MENTORING AND CONTINUING EDUCATION
Graduates can request a 30-minute mentoring session and browse advanced courses in the Alumni area. Recommendations use the graduate's stated interests and completed courses. Students can update these interests in their profile.

POLICY QUESTIONS
Consult the Training Participation and Certification Policy for attendance thresholds, assessment requirements, resits, certificate issuance times, and sharing-link expiry. This guide explains portal use and does not change those requirements.
