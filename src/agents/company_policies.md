# Company Policies

## Refund Policy
Duplicate charges are refunded within 3-5 business days to the original payment method.

## Return Policy
Products can be returned within 30 days of purchase if they are in original condition.
Refunds are processed within 7-10 business days.

## Warranty Policy
All products come with a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.
Accidental damage is not covered under warranty.

## Shipping Policy
- Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days
- Express Shipping: 2-3 business days
- Free shipping on orders over $50
